The accession links lead to the original NCBI/EBI records with full metadata. Accessions starting with PRJEB or PRJNA resolve at the EBI European Nucleotide Archive; those starting with SRP at the NCBI Sequence Read Archive. The raw data column links straight to the fast5 archive of each sample.

If a link does not start downloading in the browser, copy the link address and fetch it from a terminal instead. Archives range from a few GB to several hundred GB; use a resumable downloader:

curl -L -C - -O "https://ftp.sra.ebi.ac.uk/vol1/run/ERR276/ERR2764782/10xpolyA.tar.gz"
# or
wget -c "https://ftp.sra.ebi.ac.uk/vol1/run/ERR276/ERR2764782/10xpolyA.tar.gz"
