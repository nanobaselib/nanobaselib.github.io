format

Base calling

Production basecallers from Oxford Nanopore and research models from the community.

Dorado

ONT

Oxford Nanopore's current basecaller: DNA/RNA models, modified-base calling, polyA estimation, duplex.

github.com

Bonito

ONT

PyTorch research basecaller from Oxford Nanopore; the reference for training your own models.

github.com

Rerio

ONT

Research release models for Dorado and Bonito, including modified-base models.

github.com

Remora

ONT

Training and inference framework for modified-base detection on top of basecalling.

github.com

RODAN

research

Fully convolutional RNA basecaller from the research community.

github.com

Causalcall

research

Temporal convolutional network basecaller for DNA.

Alignment, segmentation & event alignment

Mapping reads to a reference and aligning the raw signal to k-mers, the inputs to every downstream task.

minimap2

alignment

Standard long-read aligner; map-ont and splice presets for Nanopore DNA and RNA.

github.com

Nanopolish

signal

Signal-level toolkit: eventalign, polya, methylation calling.

github.com

f5c

signal

GPU-accelerated re-implementation of Nanopolish eventalign and call-methylation.

github.com

Tombo

signal

Re-squiggle algorithm and modified-base detection on raw signal (legacy, still widely used).

nanoporetech.github.io

Uncalled4

signal

Signal alignment and visualisation toolkit with DTW-based event alignment and k-mer model training.

github.com

SegPore

signal

Raw signal segmentation and event alignment for direct RNA, with modification state estimation.

github.com

squigulator

simulation

Simulate raw Nanopore signal from a reference, useful for testing segmentation and basecalling.

github.com

RNA modification detection

Tools that call m6A, m5C, pseudouridine and other marks from direct RNA signal or basecalling errors.

m6Anet

m6A

Multiple-instance learning m6A detector on Nanopolish eventalign output.

github.com

xPore

differential

Differential modification detection between conditions from signal distributions.

github.com

CHEUI

m6A · m5C

m6A and m5C detection at single-molecule resolution with CNNs.

github.com

EpiNano

error-based

Modification detection from basecalling error features.

github.com

Nanocompore

differential

Comparative signal analysis between two samples to find modified sites.

github.com

ELIGOS

error-based

Error-of-specific-bases method; source of the eligos datasets in NanoBaseLib.

gitlab.com

modkit

ONT

Oxford Nanopore's toolkit for summarising modified-base calls (MM/ML tags) from Dorado BAMs.

github.com
